Privacy Policy — NYC Indoor Pool Finder

NYC Indoor Pool Finder — the website and the mobile app — collects no personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies set by us, and no user accounts.

The mobile app fetches pool schedule data anonymously from this site. No identifying information is sent with those requests, and nothing about you is stored.

Questions? Contact us via the GitHub repository: github.com/Think-Design-NYC/nyc-pool-finder.
